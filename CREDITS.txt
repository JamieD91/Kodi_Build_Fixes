Jamie Build Fixes packages narrowly scoped compatibility repairs for Kodi add-ons.

Original add-on code and reviewed repair fragments:
- TMDb Helper and Trakt Sync provider: jurialmunkey
  https://github.com/jurialmunkey/plugin.video.themoviedb.helper
- AutoWidget: drinfernoo
  https://github.com/drinfernoo/plugin.program.autowidget
- Umbrella: Umbrella developers
  https://umbrellaplug.github.io/
- Diggz PlaylistBrowser and Xenon/ReAuthorize integration: their respective maintainers
  https://github.com/nebulous42069/Omega
- Trakt for Kodi: Trakt add-on maintainers
  https://github.com/trakt/script.trakt
- Multi Weather, ViperScrapers and ExtendedInfo: their respective maintainers.

This toolkit is an independent personal maintenance project. It does not claim
to be an official release of any affected add-on. Original author attribution
remains in repaired files. Repairs are distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later.

No account settings, passwords, tokens, viewing logs or device addresses are
included. Local rollback copies may contain private data and remain on the
device; never upload the toolkit's userdata/backups folder to GitHub.
